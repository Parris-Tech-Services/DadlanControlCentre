DadLAN subnetting lab

In the documented September 2026 incident, devices #03 and #07 had addresses in 10.176.176.0/24 while the LANCommander server was on 10.245.173.58. The repair moved the clients to the DadLAN network and restored connectivity.

Question: With a /24 mask, are 10.176.176.20 and 10.245.173.58 in the same subnet?

No. A /24 mask is 255.255.255.0, so the network addresses are 10.176.176.0 and 10.245.173.0. They require routing to communicate. The historical repair also involved network configuration and service reachability; subnet mismatch alone does not prove the whole cause.

Continue the practice

  1. DCSPD: explain how /24 relates to 255.255.255.0.
  2. Avance: write a customer-friendly ticket noting IP, mask, gateway, DNS and TCP/1337 tests.
  3. DCS: use a troubleshooting scenario to distinguish subnet errors from firewall or service failures.
  4. DadLAN: revisit the historical incident and safely plan a read-only test: ipconfig /all, ping and Test-NetConnection.
